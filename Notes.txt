EDA means looking at and understanding your dataset before doing further analysis or machine learning.
    
    EDA = Exploratory Data Analysis
    Spread → How far the values are from each other.
    Outlier : An outlier is a value that is unusually far from the rest of the data.
    Histogram → "How are my values distributed/Frequency?"
    Box plot  → "How spread out are my values, and are there unusual values?"
    bins -> divides the range into a specified number of smaller intervals (bins).
